Hospital Near Ompora Railway Station

Ibn Sina Hospital is directly adjacent to Ompora Railway Station on the Banihal–Baramulla line. 24/7 OPD, emergency, dialysis, and PM-JAY cashless treatment — easy to reach by train.

Why Our Location Matters

If you or a loved one is arriving by train from Srinagar, Budgam, Baramulla, Anantnag, or Banihal, Ibn Sina Hospital is quite literally next to the platform. No long commute, no unfamiliar bus routes — just step off and you're here.

Getting Here by Train

Ompora Railway Station is on the Banihal–Baramulla line. Regular trains stop here from the following stations:

1

From Srinagar

Approximately 20 minutes by train. Step off at Ompora — the hospital is adjacent to the platform exit.

2

From Budgam

Approximately 10 minutes by train. Ompora is one stop south of Budgam railway station.

3

From Baramulla

Approximately 1 hour by train along the Kashmir valley rail line.

4

From Anantnag / Banihal

Approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours by train depending on the starting station.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ibn Sina Hospital adjacent to Ompora Railway Station?
