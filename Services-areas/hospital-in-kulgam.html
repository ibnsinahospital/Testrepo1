Getting Here from Kulgam

Ibn Sina Hospital is in Ompora, Budgam — approximately 70 km from Kulgam town, about 1 hour 45 minutes by car via NH-44 towards Srinagar, then Budgam Road to Ompora.

1

By Car

Take NH-44 north through Anantnag and Awantipora. Before Srinagar, turn onto Budgam Road for Ompora.

2

By Train

Trains on the Banihal–Baramulla line stop at Ompora. The hospital is adjacent to the station.

3

By Bus

Regular buses run between Kulgam and Budgam via Anantnag and Srinagar.

4

By Ambulance

For emergencies, call 9622552553 to arrange transport.
