Getting Here from Ganderbal

Ibn Sina Hospital is located in Ompora, Budgam — approximately 35 km from Ganderbal town, about 1 hour by car. Most patients from Ganderbal travel via Srinagar and then NH-44 towards Budgam, or via the Budgam Road route. The hospital is right next to the Ompora Railway Station.

1

Via Srinagar

Take the Srinagar–Budgam road to Ompora. Total drive from Ganderbal is around 1 to 1.5 hours depending on traffic through Srinagar.

2

Via Budgam Road

Route via Shalteng, then Budgam Road, then Ompora Railway Station Road. Follow signs for Ompora Railway Station.

3

By Train

Ompora has its own railway station on the Banihal–Baramulla line. Patients can travel by train and the hospital is adjacent to the station.

4

By Ambulance

For emergencies, call 9622552553 — our ambulance can be dispatched for urgent cases.

Frequently Asked Questions — Ganderbal

Does Ibn Sina Hospital accept patients from Ganderbal?
Yes, we regularly welcome patients from Ganderbal district for OPD consultations, dialysis, diagnostics, and emergency care. Our team can guide you on travel and appointment scheduling by phone before you visit.
How far is Ibn Sina Hospital from Ganderbal?
Ibn Sina Hospital is in Ompora, Budgam — approximately 35 km from Ganderbal town, about 1 hour by car via Srinagar and NH-44. The hospital is next to the Ompora Railway Station. View on Google Maps →
Is dialysis available for Ganderbal patients?
Yes. Ibn Sina Hospital runs an in-house hemodialysis unit — the only one of its kind in Budgam district. We welcome dialysis patients from Ganderbal and surrounding areas. Call 9622552553 to schedule. Learn more →
Is PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat cashless treatment available for Ganderbal patients?
Yes. Ibn Sina Hospital is a PM-JAY empanelled hospital. Eligible patients from Ganderbal can avail cashless treatment under the Ayushman Bharat scheme. Learn more →
Do you have emergency and ambulance support for patients coming from Ganderbal?
Yes, our emergency department and ambulance service operate 24/7, 365 days a year. Call 9622552553 for urgent situations.
Which specialties are most useful for Ganderbal patients?
Nephrology (dialysis and kidney care), cardiology, gynaecology, orthopaedics, gastroenterology, and general medicine are the most commonly used services by patients travelling from Ganderbal.
