Frequently Asked Questions — Budgam

Where exactly is Ibn Sina Hospital in Budgam?
Ibn Sina Hospital is located at Ompora, near the Railway Station on Ompora Railway Station Road, Budgam, J&K 191111. View on Google Maps →
Does Ibn Sina Hospital have dialysis in Budgam?
Yes. Ibn Sina Hospital runs an in-house hemodialysis unit — the only one of its kind in Budgam district. Call 9622552553 to check availability and scheduling. Learn more about Nephrology →
Is PM-JAY accepted at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Yes. Ibn Sina Hospital is PM-JAY empanelled, and eligible families can receive cashless treatment. Visit our Ayushman Mitra desk for eligibility check. Learn more →
What emergency services are available in Budgam?
24/7 emergency department, ambulance service, pharmacy, diagnostic lab, and consultant backup — open every day of the year.
Which specialties are available at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Cardiology, nephrology, orthopaedics, gynaecology, urology, gastroenterology, general surgery, general medicine, ENT, ophthalmology, dermatology, pulmonology, rheumatology, plastic surgery, paediatric surgery, radiology, physiotherapy, dentistry, NICU, and more.
