What PM-JAY Covers at Ibn Sina Hospital

PM-JAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana) covers a wide range of secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. At Ibn Sina, eligible patients can access:

  • Emergency admissions — acute conditions, trauma, cardiac events
  • Dialysis sessions — including ongoing treatment for kidney failure
  • General surgery — hernia, gallbladder, appendix, and other procedures
  • Gynaecology & maternity — delivery, C-section, and gynaecological surgeries
  • Orthopaedics — fracture management, joint procedures, trauma
  • Gastroenterology procedures — endoscopy, colonoscopy, therapeutic interventions
  • Cardiology diagnostics — TMT, Holter, ABPM, ECG, ECHO
  • Diagnostics — lab tests, imaging, and pathology
  • Prescribed medications during hospitalisation

Who Is Eligible for PM-JAY?

PM-JAY eligibility is based on the SECC 2011 database. In Jammu & Kashmir, eligible families are identified through:

  • Rural areas: families classified as deprived or automatically included
  • Urban areas: families in specific occupational categories
  • Verification possible at our Ayushman Mitra desk or online

If you're unsure about your eligibility, call us. We can walk you through the check over the phone.

How to Use PM-JAY at Ibn Sina Hospital

  1. Bring your PM-JAY card (Golden Card) or Aadhaar to the hospital.
  2. Visit our Ayushman Mitra desk on arrival — they verify eligibility.
  3. Receive treatment without any upfront payment for covered procedures.
  4. We handle the paperwork and file the claim on your behalf.

Important: Always carry your PM-JAY e-card and Aadhaar when you visit. If you don't have your card yet, we can guide you on how to obtain one.
