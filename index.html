A Shelter of Healing in Budgam

Since 2018, Ibn Sina Hospital has been a trusted multi-specialty hospital in Budgam, J&K. Our consultant-led model ensures expert care for every patient — from 24/7 OPD to advanced diagnostics.

We combine a 24/7 pharmacy, round-the-clock laboratory, dialysis, endoscopy, TMT, Holter, and ABPM under one roof, so families don't have to travel between centres for the care they need.
